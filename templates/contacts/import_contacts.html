Expected CSV Headers & Format

Your CSV must have a header row with column names. The importer is flexible with column casing and common alias names.

Column Required Description / Example
first_name Yes Contact's first name (e.g., Sarah)
last_name No Contact's last name (e.g., Connor)
phone At least 1 method Primary contact number (e.g., +1 555-234-5678)
email At least 1 method Valid email address (e.g., sarah@cyberdyne.io)
alternate_phone No Secondary or mobile number
company No Organization name (e.g., Cyberdyne Systems)
job_title No Position or role (e.g., Chief Security Officer)
category No Work, Family, Friends, Clients, University, Other (or custom)
city No City or Region (e.g., San Francisco)
birthday No Date in YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY format
notes No Free-text notes and descriptions
